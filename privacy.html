Privacy policy

Personal Tasks is used only by its owner, with their own Google account.

  • It accesses Google Tasks and Google Calendar data solely to show and update that data on the owner's own computer.
  • The data and the authorization token stay on that computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs, and nothing is shared with third parties.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind is used.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
